## 1.0.0

- Auto-corrects keyword, standard-library and document-identifier casing as
  you type, with no acceptance step.
- Verified support for Java, C, C++, JavaScript, TypeScript (incl. JSX/TSX),
  Python, Rust, C#, Kotlin, Swift, PHP, Ruby, Go, SQL, Lua, R and shell.
- Conservative design: ambiguous or unknown words are never changed;
  strings and comments are never touched; undoing a fix suppresses it.
- `TrueCase: Fix Casing in This File` command for whole-file sweeps.
- Status bar toggle, per-rule settings and user-supplied symbol tables.
